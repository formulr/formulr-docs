# Integrated messaging

Integrated messaging centralizes conversations **directly inside each client dossier**. Two types of communication are available:

| Type | Who talks | Visible to the client |
|------|-----------|-----------------------|
| **Internal messaging** | You and your collaborators | No |
| **Client messaging** | You and the client | Yes |

The "Unread messages" counter on the home screen helps you spot new questions from your clients.

::: info Screenshots
The screenshots below show the interface in French.
:::

## Internal messaging

Talk to your collaborators within the dossier, without the client seeing the messages.

1. **Open the dossier:** scroll down to the "History" section.
2. **Write a comment:** type your text in "Leave an internal comment...", then click "Publish".
3. **Mention a collaborator:** type "@" (or click the "@" button) to pick a collaborator. They receive a notification.

![Internal comment with a mention in the dossier history](/screenshots/messaging/internal-comment.png)

::: info Confidentiality
Only you and the other collaborators can see these comments.
:::

## Client messaging

To start a new discussion with the client, click "Messages" in the right column of the client record, under "Client information".

![Messages button in the client record](/screenshots/messaging/messages-button.png)

## Client side: asking a question

The client has two ways to write to you. Their messages arrive in the relevant dossier.

### Message on a module

To ask a question about a specific module, the client clicks the message bubble next to that module.

![Message bubble next to a module, client side](/screenshots/messaging/client-module-bubble.png)

**Administrator side:** the message appears in the dossier history, with the name of the module concerned. Click it to open the conversation and reply directly to the client. Unread messages are flagged "Unread".

![Client message in the dossier history](/screenshots/messaging/admin-module-message.png)

![Conversation opened with the module concerned](/screenshots/messaging/admin-conversation.png)

### Global message

The client can ask a general question by clicking the "A question?" button at the bottom right of their screen. They find your answer in the same messaging.

![A question? button, client side](/screenshots/messaging/client-global-question.png)

**Administrator side:** the message appears in the client dossier. Click it to open the conversation and reply to the client.
