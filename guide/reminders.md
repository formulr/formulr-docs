# Reminders

A reminder sends the client an **email containing a link** inviting them to complete their dossier. You can automate it for a whole campaign, exclude specific dossiers, or remind a client manually.

The process has three stages:

1. Enable automatic reminders in the campaign.
2. Exclude a specific dossier if needed.
3. Send a manual reminder when necessary.

::: info Screenshots
The screenshots below show the interface in French.
:::

## Enable automatic reminders

1. **Open the settings:** in the campaign, click the "Settings" tab.
2. **Enable:** in the "Automatic reminder" section, turn on "Enable automatic reminders". The reminder then applies to all dossiers of the campaign.
3. **Set the frequency:** choose "Once a day", "Every 2 days", "Once a week" or "Every 2 weeks".
4. **Limit the number of sends:** set the maximum number of times.

![Automatic reminder settings in the campaign settings](/screenshots/reminders/campaign-settings.png)

Reminders ask recipients to finish the current step of their dossier. The email content is customized like the other automatic emails (see [Account and collaborators](/guide/account#automatic-emails-of-a-campaign)).

## Exclude a dossier from reminders

To exclude a specific dossier, open it and turn off the "Automatic reminders" option in the right panel, under "Campaign information". The other dossiers keep receiving reminders.

This panel also shows the date of the last reminder and how many reminders have already been sent (for example "2 / 2 sent").

![Automatic reminders option in a client's dossier](/screenshots/reminders/dossier-reminders.png)

## Send a manual reminder

You can remind a client at any time, independently of automatic reminders.

1. **Open the menu:** in the client's dossier, click the ⋮ menu at the top right.
2. **Remind:** under "Links and access", click "Send a reminder".

The same menu also lets you "Copy the client access link" to send it yourself.

![Send a reminder menu](/screenshots/reminders/manual-reminder.png)

::: tip Closed dossier
A closed dossier ("To validate", "Validated" or "Rejected") can no longer be edited by the client. To let them complete it, reopen it first with "Revise the response" (see [Annexes](/guide/annexes)).
:::
