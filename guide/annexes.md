# Annexes: asking the client for additional information

An **annex** lets you ask a client for a document or a piece of information that was not planned in the campaign. It only applies to the dossier it is created on: the campaign and the other dossiers are not modified.

**Typical case:** the client has submitted their dossier (status "To validate"), but a document or some information is missing.

The process has three stages:

1. Create the annex in the dossier.
2. Reopen the dossier to the client.
3. The client completes the annex and submits the dossier again.

## Create an annex

1. **Open the Annex section:** in the dossier, scroll down to the "Team space", open the "Annex" section, then click "Add an annex".

   ![Annex section of the Team space](/screenshots/annexes/a02.png)

2. **Choose what to request:** document, short or long text, number, date, yes / no, contact information...

   ![Choosing the element type](/screenshots/annexes/a03.png)

## Configure the requested elements

Each element of the annex is configured like a campaign module.

3. **Label:** click in the module and type the request (e.g. "Proof of address less than 3 months old").
4. **Required:** click the module's gear icon and enable "Required".
5. **Save:** add more elements with "Add a module" if needed, then click "Save".

![Module settings: Required](/screenshots/annexes/a05.png)

![Annex with two elements and the Save button](/screenshots/annexes/a06.png)

::: warning Important
Make at least one element required. Otherwise the client is not alerted by the "Additional information" banner; the annex is only reachable from their summary.
:::

## Reopen the dossier to the client

Until the dossier is reopened, the client only sees the thank-you page and cannot complete the annex.

6. **Reopen the dossier:** click the arrow next to "Validate the response", then "Revise the response". The client automatically receives an email inviting them to make changes to their dossier.

   ![Revise the response menu](/screenshots/annexes/a08.png)

   The dossier goes back to the "In progress" status: the client can access it again.

   ![In progress status](/screenshots/annexes/a09.png)

## Client side: completing the annex

1. **Alert:** when opening their dossier, the client sees the "Additional information" banner.

   ![Additional information banner](/screenshots/annexes/c01.png)

2. **Input:** clicking it opens the "Annexes" panel. The client uploads the documents, fills in the information, then clicks "Send".

   ![Completed Annexes panel](/screenshots/annexes/c03.png)

3. **New submission:** the client then clicks "Validate" to submit the dossier again.

   ![Send confirmation and Validate button](/screenshots/annexes/c04.png)

## Review the submitted information

- **Dossier back:** once submitted again, the dossier returns to the "To validate" status.
- **View the annex:** in the "Team space", the "Annex" section shows the documents and information sent by the client.
- **Decide:** then validate or reject the dossier as usual.

![Dossier back to the To validate status](/screenshots/annexes/a10.png)
